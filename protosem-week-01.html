My First Week at ProTosem

Week One — Design Thinking, Analytics, and Practical Innovation

Rithika H
Innovation Engineer Trainee • PRICE ProtoSem (Batch 2026)

Weekly Reflection & Overview

My first week at ProTosem was an exciting journey filled with learning, creativity, and new experiences. We explored topics like Instagram algorithms, Design Thinking, Power BI, SWOT analysis, Prospect Theory, portfolio building, and application development.

Our pharmacy-sector project helped us apply these concepts through teamwork and practical activities. We also gained valuable insights from our mentors and alumni about career paths and future opportunities. Overall, the week helped me think differently, learn practically, and discover new perspectives.

Key Topics & Frameworks Explored

Design Thinking & App Development

Learned human-centric design methodologies: empathizing with end-users, defining core pain points, rapid prototyping, and conceptualizing software solutions that solve real industry problems.

Power BI & Business Intelligence

Explored data visualization tools to transform complex business information into intuitive interactive dashboards for commercial and operational insights.

SWOT Analysis & Prospect Theory

Delved into behavioral economics and strategic risk management—analyzing how psychological biases and market risks shape consumer behavior and organizational decision-making.

Instagram Algorithms & Portfolio Building

Examined social media distribution algorithms, personal branding strategies, and professional web portfolio curation to showcase innovation projects to employers and founders.

Hands-On Application
Team Innovation Project

Pharmacy-Sector Applied Project

We bridged theoretical concepts with real-world practice by tackling challenges in the pharmacy and healthcare retail sector. Applying SWOT analysis and Design Thinking frameworks, our team collaborated on mapping customer journeys, identifying supply chain bottlenecks, and designing structured digital workflows.

Empathy Mapping
Understanding pharmacy retail consumers
Strategic SWOT
Evaluating market risks and opportunities
Solution Modeling
Prototyping practical application features

Mentorship & Alumni Career Perspectives

Beyond technical modules, we had the privilege of engaging with experienced mentors and ProtoSem alumni. They shared actionable advice regarding career trajectories, technological convergence in commerce and finance, and how to maximize the 20-week innovation journey.

"The mentorship interactions opened my eyes to diverse career pathways, reminding us that continuous curiosity and multidisciplinary skills are key to building impactful solutions."

Weekly Synthesis

Looking Ahead to Week Two

Week One gave me the tools to think differently, learn practically, and discover new perspectives. Ready to dive deeper into product development and innovation!
