My First Week at Protosem

Week One – A Journey of Learning, Creativity and New Perspectives

Rithika H
Innovation Engineer Trainee • PRICE ProtoSem (Batch 2026)

Week One – Learning Through Experience

Week One at Protosem was an exciting journey filled with technology, creativity, teamwork, industry exploration, and practical learning. Every session introduced us to a new idea and encouraged us to connect what we learned with real-world situations.

From understanding how Instagram works to exploring the pharmacy industry, learning Power BI, studying Prospect Theory, building a portfolio, developing an application, and thinking about our future vision, the week gave me many valuable experiences.

Day-by-Day Journey

DAY 01 Tech Talk & Creative Thinking

Day 1 – Instagram and Design Thinking

The first session was a tech talk by Vedika about Instagram, its feed, and how Instagram decides what users see. We learned how the platform uses user interests and behaviour to make the feed more relevant. It was interesting to understand the technology behind something we use every day.

Later, we had a session with Meera Ma'am on Design Thinking. She gave us a creative task: if someone had thirty circles, what could they do with them? There were no restrictions, so we were encouraged to think beyond the obvious. Our team chose the pharmacy sector and created a poster based on our ideas. Choosing pharmacy helped us explore how creativity and design thinking could be applied to a real industry.

DAY 02 Analytics & Sector Valuation

Day 2 – Power BI, Value Chain and SWOT Analysis

On the second day, Afrina introduced us to Power BI. We learned how data can be represented visually and how data visualization can support better understanding and decision-making.

We also worked on the value chain and SWOT analysis of the pharmacy sector. Studying the value chain helped us understand the different stages involved in the pharmaceutical industry, while the SWOT analysis helped us identify its strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. We presented our work in front of Balu Sir and Suganya Ma'am, which helped us improve our communication and presentation skills.

DAY 03 Behavioral Economics & Antigravity Portfolio

Day 3 – Prospect Theory and Portfolio Building

On the third day, Parameshwari conducted a tech talk on Prospect Theory. The session was very useful because it helped us understand how people make decisions when they are faced with gains and losses. It gave us a different perspective on human behaviour and decision-making.

We also had a portfolio session where we learned how to present our skills, experiences, and interests. We used Antigravity for the portfolio activity, which gave us an opportunity to explore a new way of building our personal portfolio.

DAY 04 Prompt Engineering & Future Pathways

Day 4 – Application Development and Finding Our Vision

The fourth day began with a tech talk by Linga Raj on Base44 and application development. We did not just learn the concept theoretically; we also developed an application through prompts. This activity showed us how technology and AI tools can help turn an idea into a working application.

Vision Session with Ram Sir & Alumni Interactions

After that, we had a session with Ram Sir about vision. He encouraged us to choose our path according to our own thoughts, interests, and aspirations. We also interacted with our alumni from industries such as agriculture and textiles. Their experiences gave us useful insights into different career journeys and possibilities.

CULMINATION Cohort Presentations

Weekly Presentation – Bringing Our Learning Together

At the end of the week, we had our weekly presentation. It gave us an opportunity to bring together the work we had completed during the week and share our learning. The presentation also helped us reflect on our teamwork, communication, and ability to explain our ideas clearly.

What I Learned From Week One

Week One taught me that learning becomes more meaningful when we apply it through activities and real examples. I learned to think creatively, work with a team, understand an industry from different perspectives, communicate ideas, and explore new technology. The sessions also encouraged me to think about my future and the direction I want to take.

Creative Problem Solving

Applying 30-circles Design Thinking to the Indian Pharmacy retail sector.

Data Visualization & SWOT

Leveraging Power BI dashboards and strategic industry value-chain mapping.

AI Application Prototyping

Prompt-driven app building with Base44 and personal portfolio development in Antigravity.

Vision & Career Insights

Prospect Theory decision-making models and alumni interactions from textiles and agriculture.

Closing Reflection

A Week to Remember

My first week at Protosem was filled with new ideas, practical activities, presentations, and valuable interactions. Each session added something different to my learning journey. Week One gave me a strong start and made me curious about what the coming weeks will bring.
