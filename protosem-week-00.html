My First Week at Protosem

Week Zero — A Journey of Learning, Teamwork and New Experiences

Rithika H
Innovation Engineer Trainee • PRICE ProtoSem (Batch 2026)

The Beginning of My Protosem Journey

Week Zero was an exciting start to my Protosem journey, filled with fun activities, games, and new experiences. It helped us interact with new people, break the ice, and build strong connections with our teammates.

Through various challenges and team activities, we learned the importance of communication, creativity, and teamwork. The week also helped us understand our personalities, identify real-world problems, and explore our strengths. Overall, Week Zero gave us a strong foundation and prepared us for an exciting journey of learning, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Day-by-Day Experience

DAY 01 Icebreaking & Problem Identification

Breaking the Ice and Getting to Know Each Other

The first day was all about getting comfortable in a new environment and interacting with new people. We participated in several interesting games and activities that helped us introduce ourselves and build connections.

Key Activity: Problem Identification

We identified three problem statements that we were facing in our daily contexts, which encouraged us to start thinking critically about real-world problems and possible practical solutions.

DAY 02 Self-Discovery & Professional Identity

Understanding Myself Better

On the second day, we had a LinkedIn orientation that helped us understand how to build our professional identity and present our career aspirations effectively.

16 Personalities Discovery: Protagonist (ENFJ)

I took the 16 Personalities assessment and discovered my personality type is Protagonist (ENFJ). As part of the activity, we read a story related to our personality type and shared reflections with peers, allowing me to understand my strengths in leadership, empathy, communication, and collaborative teamwork.

DAY 03 Team Dynamics & Prototyping

Team Formation and the Marshmallow Challenge

The third day was one of the most exciting days because we formed our Beta teams. We participated in interactive games such as the Imposter game and other dynamic group challenges.

One of the most memorable activities was the Marshmallow Challenge, where we worked with spaghetti, thread, tape, and a marshmallow to build the tallest freestanding structure. The challenge taught us the critical importance of rapid prototyping, communication, creativity, planning, and teamwork under time constraints.

DAY 04 Forge Ecosystem & Leadership Vision

Understanding Forge

On the fourth day, we had a session with Hema, where we learned about the rules, regulations, professional conduct, and expectations of Forge.

In the afternoon, we attended the official orientation of Forge and listened to the special address by Kumar Rajagopalan. The session gave us a deep understanding of the organization, its innovation vision, and the vast opportunities available to us as Innovation Engineer Trainees.

DAY 05 YEP Kickoff & Entrepreneurial Inspiration

YEP Orientation and Entrepreneurial Inspiration

The fifth day was another milestone experience as we attended the YEP (Young Entrepreneurship Program) orientation. It was also an inspiring Entrepreneur Day, where we had the opportunity to listen directly to an entrepreneur share the candid journey of building an organization from scratch.

The session provided valuable first-hand insights into leadership, resilience, problem-solving, market determination, and entrepreneurial mindset.

What I Learned From My First Week

Looking back at my first week at Protosem, I feel that it was much more than an orientation program. Every activity had a purpose. I learned how important it is to communicate with new people, understand myself better, work effectively as part of a team, and approach problems creatively.

Team Collaboration

Forming Beta teams and solving the Marshmallow Challenge reinforced collective brainstorming and mutual support.

Self-Awareness (ENFJ)

Understanding my Protagonist personality type helped clarify my strengths in communication and empathy.

Hands-on Problem Solving

Real learning happens beyond textbooks—through rapid prototyping, creative constraints, and experimentation.

Entrepreneurial Mindset

Listening to founder journeys inspired resilience, customer-centric problem solving, and long-term vision.

Closing Reflection

A Week to Remember

My first week at Protosem was filled with new people, new challenges, new ideas, and new learning experiences. From ice-breaking games and personality discovery to team challenges, Forge orientation, and entrepreneurial sessions, every day added something new to my journey. Week Zero gave me a strong foundation, and I am excited to see what the coming weeks bring.
